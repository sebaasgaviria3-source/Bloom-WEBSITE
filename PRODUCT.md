# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/vanilla JS, no build step, uploaded as-is to Hostinger. Libraries allowed: GSAP + ScrollTrigger + Lenis (in `lib/`). No others without asking the owner.

## Users

People in Vigo (and visitors) deciding where to have brunch or coffee, mostly on their phone: they want to see the food and the place, know if it is open now, and find the address, phone or WhatsApp quickly.

## Product Purpose

The website of Bloom Coffee & Brunch, Calle Ángel Llanos 10, 36203 Vigo. It shows the food, the venue and the brand, and turns visits into footfall and social followers. Success: visitors come in, call or message, and follow Bloom on social media.

## Positioning

A pink, floral brunch café with a teddy-bear mascot, specialty coffee, açaí bowls and Brazilian food (coxinhas, pastéis, pão de queijo).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Content in Spanish; code in English.
- Opening hours: Monday–Friday 9:00–22:00, Saturday 10:00–23:00, Sunday closed. A live open/closed status (Europe/Madrid) is shown.
- Contact: +34 640 24 01 76 (phone and WhatsApp), bloomcoffeeb@gmail.com, Instagram @bloom.coffee.vigo.
- The menu (dishes and prices) is not published yet: only a "MENÚ / Muy pronto" cover. Do not invent dishes or prices.
- The story text is provisional until the owner sends the final copy.
- Legal pages (aviso legal, privacidad, cookies) are pending.
- Social profiles other than Instagram and WhatsApp (TikTok, Pinterest, Facebook, YouTube) have no URLs yet.

## Brand Commitments

- Logo: `assets/img/logo-bloom.webp`; the neon "Bloom / Coffee" sign in the hero is part of the logo.
- Mascot: the line-art teddy bear holding a rose, reused across the page.
- Owner-pinned visual rules: strong pink #E0356B only in the hero; pastels (soft pink, lavender, peach, cream) elsewhere; cocoa #4A2433 text. Typography: Poppins ExtraBold for headings and Inter for text; Allison only for the logo script and the matching social slogan.
- Nothing copied from other sites; only motion behaviour may be taken from references.

## Evidence on Hand

Real photographs of the food and venue in `assets/photos/source/` (processed by `tools/build_images.py` into `assets/img/`). No testimonials, press or awards: do not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

- The real photos and the real place sell Bloom; design frames them, never hides them.
- Practical info (open now, address, call, WhatsApp) is always one tap away.
- Playful but premium: the bear and florals add warmth without looking childish.
- Fast on mobile data: Lighthouse mobile ≥ 90.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA contrast and focus; full static version under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
